What’s New

From the search screen to the results list, from the dictionary page to the Word Solver: what the new version brings.

With LexiQamus 3.0 every screen of the tool has been redesigned. Below are the highlights of the new version; each is explained in detail in the User Guide (currently in Turkish).

Search

Two ways to search

The Search tab looks up a word you can read; the Word Solver identifies a word you cannot read, starting from the clues you have.

A search box in two scripts

Search within English definitions

With ENG selected, the search runs inside the English definitions of the dictionaries; every entry whose definition contains the English word is listed.

Ottoman on-screen keyboard

Choice of dictionaries

Results list

Results with their sources

The list opens with how many records were found and how many dictionaries and volumes were scanned; each row shows the dictionary and page number the record comes from.

In the guide

Search by spelling

The likely spellings and readings of what you typed appear as buttons. These are not guesses; they are data entered while digitizing the dictionaries.

In the guide

Word categories

Every result is labeled Headword, Subentry or Related; the colored bar on the left of the row marks the same distinction.

In the guide

Editor-approved readings

You can see which Latin readings were approved by editors and which were generated automatically; mark a reading as Correct or report a correction.

In the guide

Two click zones

The left side of a row takes you to where the word occurs, the right side to the start of the entry; clicking a word in the row searches it in a new tab.

In the guide

Ready-made citations

The citation for every record is copied with one click in APA, Chicago and Harvard styles.

In the guide

Scan Similar Matches

If you cannot find what you searched for, words close to your spelling letter by letter appear as a numbered list.

In the guide

Dictionary page

The dictionary as an image

The entry opens on the original scan of the dictionary. Digitized words sit in colored boxes; hover to see the Ottoman spelling and the reading, click to start a new search.

In the guide

Slice, column and page

View the entry on its own, with its whole column, or with the whole page; use the arrows to move through the dictionary entry by entry, column by column or page by page.

In the guide

Error reporting

Every digitized word has a warning sign next to it; when you spot an error, report it from there.

In the guide

Word Solver

Basic and advanced keyboard

The basic keyboard is enough for every search; the advanced keyboard offers every written form of the letters and the zero-width character on separate keys.

In the guide

Typing with your own keyboard

You do not have to click the keys; you can also type the letters straight into the boxes with your own keyboard.

In the guide

Solver results

The Solver’s results come in the new layout too. Thanks to the digital dictionary ecosystem we built in BuildLQ, records are grouped under headings that run from the root to phrases, from the simplest form to the broadest; the rows behave just like those in the Search tab.
